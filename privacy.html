Privacy policy

Life Dashboard is a personal app used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

With the owner's permission it reads the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar to display them, and moves junk email to the Trash when the owner asks. It never sends email or deletes anything permanently.

Data stays on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone. Short email previews may be sent to an AI service to help sort junk, only at the owner's direction.

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
